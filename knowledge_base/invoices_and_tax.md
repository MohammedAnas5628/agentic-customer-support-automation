# ElectroMart Invoices and Tax Policy

## Overview

ElectroMart provides invoices for eligible purchases and records
applicable taxes according to the transaction and product information.

This document explains invoice availability, GST information, business
invoices, billing details, and invoice corrections.

The generated invoice and order system are the source of truth for the
exact tax amount, invoice number, taxable value, and customer billing
information.

## Invoice Availability

An invoice is generated for a completed eligible order according to
ElectroMart's billing process.

The customer may receive or access the invoice through the applicable
order/account channel.

Customer support must not invent invoice numbers or tax amounts.

## GST Invoice

Where applicable, ElectroMart's invoice includes relevant GST
information, which may include:

-   Seller information.
-   Customer information.
-   GSTIN where applicable.
-   Invoice number.
-   Invoice date.
-   Product description.
-   Quantity.
-   Taxable value.
-   Applicable tax components.
-   Total invoice value.

The exact tax treatment depends on the product, transaction, and
applicable Indian tax rules.

## GSTIN for Business Customers

Customers purchasing for a business may request that their valid GSTIN
be associated with the transaction where ElectroMart's billing process
permits it.

Customers should provide the correct GSTIN and business details before
invoice generation whenever possible.

A GSTIN must not be fabricated or altered by support.

## GSTIN After Order Placement

A customer should request a GSTIN or billing-information correction as
soon as possible.

Whether the GSTIN can be added or changed after order placement depends
on the invoice-generation stage and applicable billing controls.

After a tax invoice has been finalized, changes may require
cancellation/reissue or may not be permitted.

Support must check the billing system before promising a correction.

## Billing Address and Delivery Address

The billing address and delivery address can be different.

Changing the delivery address does not automatically change the billing
information.

An invoice correction must be processed through the authorized billing
workflow.

## Invoice Corrections

A correction request may be considered for:

-   Typographical errors.
-   Incorrect billing details.
-   Eligible GSTIN corrections before finalization.
-   Other documented billing discrepancies.

Support must not manually edit a finalized tax invoice outside the
approved billing process.

## Business Invoices

A business customer should provide accurate:

-   Legal business name.
-   GSTIN where applicable.
-   Billing address.
-   Required tax information.

ElectroMart may reject a correction when the requested information
cannot be verified or when the invoice stage does not permit changes.

## Invoice and Refund Relationship

A refund changes the financial outcome of an order but does not
necessarily mean that the original invoice disappears.

Where a refund, cancellation, or return requires a financial document or
invoice adjustment, the billing system determines the appropriate
record.

Customers should use the invoice and refund records provided by
ElectroMart rather than calculating tax adjustments themselves.

## Discounts and Tax

Promotional discounts may affect the taxable value and final invoice
amount according to the transaction's billing calculation.

The customer should use the generated invoice as the authoritative
record for the actual tax and discount amounts.

## Invoice and COD

COD customers are entitled to the applicable invoice for an eligible
purchase.

The payment method does not by itself remove the customer's right to
receive an applicable invoice.

## Missing Invoice

If a customer cannot find an invoice:

1.  Verify the order.
2.  Check whether the invoice has been generated.
3.  Provide the authorized invoice-access or retrieval process.
4.  Escalate to billing support if the invoice should exist but is
    unavailable.

Support must not create an unofficial invoice.

## Invoice Edge Cases

### Customer wants GSTIN added after delivery

The request should be checked against invoice-finalization status. Do
not guarantee that a finalized invoice can be modified.

### Customer gives an invalid GSTIN

The billing information should not be accepted as valid without the
required verification.

### Billing and shipping addresses differ

This is allowed where the checkout and billing rules support it. The two
addresses should not be treated as identical by default.

### Customer wants a different tax amount

Support should refer to the generated invoice and billing records rather
than manually calculating a replacement tax amount.
