# ElectroMart Warranty Policy

## Overview

A warranty is protection against qualifying product defects or failures
during the applicable warranty period.

Warranty coverage is different from a return or replacement window.

-   A **return** allows an eligible delivered product to be sent back
    under the return policy.
-   A **replacement** provides another product for a qualifying issue
    within the applicable replacement process.
-   A **warranty claim** addresses a qualifying product fault during the
    applicable warranty period.

The warranty provider and exact coverage depend on the product.

## Standard Warranty Periods

Unless a product listing, invoice, or warranty record specifies a
different period, the following standard periods apply:

  Product category                                   Standard warranty
  ------------------------------- ------------------------------------
  Laptops and desktop computers                                 1 year
  Monitors                                                      1 year
  Smartphones and tablets                                       1 year
  Networking equipment                                          1 year
  Computer accessories                                        6 months
  Chargers and power adapters                                 6 months
  Audio products                                                1 year
  Other electronics                 As specified in the product record

The product-specific warranty record is authoritative when it differs
from the general category period.

The warranty period normally begins on the invoice/order delivery date
recorded by ElectroMart, unless the applicable manufacturer warranty
terms specify another start date.

## Warranty Providers

A product may have:

1.  **Manufacturer warranty** --- the manufacturer is responsible for
    warranty service according to its warranty terms.
2.  **ElectroMart warranty** --- ElectroMart is responsible for the
    stated warranty service.
3.  **Limited or category-specific coverage** --- the product record
    defines the applicable responsibility.

Customer support must verify the warranty provider for the specific
product before promising repair, replacement, or other service.

## Manufacturer Warranty

For manufacturer-covered products, the manufacturer may control:

-   Diagnostic procedures.
-   Repair authorization.
-   Service-center selection.
-   Replacement decisions.
-   Parts availability.
-   Warranty exclusions.

ElectroMart may help the customer understand the process or direct the
customer to the authorized manufacturer support channel.

ElectroMart does not guarantee that a manufacturer will approve a claim.

## ElectroMart Warranty

Where ElectroMart is the stated warranty provider, ElectroMart handles
the warranty claim according to the applicable product terms.

The available remedy may include:

-   Repair.
-   Replacement.
-   Approved component replacement.
-   Another authorized remedy.

The specific remedy depends on product condition, availability,
diagnosis, and applicable warranty terms.

## Warranty Coverage

A warranty generally covers qualifying manufacturing defects or
functional failures that occur during the applicable warranty period.

Examples may include:

-   Device fails to power on without evidence of external damage.
-   Display develops a qualifying internal hardware fault.
-   Internal component fails under normal use.
-   Product stops functioning because of a covered manufacturing defect.

Warranty coverage does not mean every failure during the warranty period
is automatically covered.

## Warranty Exclusions

Warranty coverage generally does not include:

-   Accidental damage.
-   Cracked screens caused by impact.
-   Liquid or moisture damage where excluded by the product terms.
-   Damage caused by misuse.
-   Damage caused by incorrect installation or unsafe electrical
    conditions.
-   Unauthorized repair or modification.
-   Cosmetic wear that does not affect functionality.
-   Damage caused by incompatible accessories or external equipment
    where applicable.
-   Missing or altered serial numbers.
-   Software issues unrelated to a covered hardware defect, unless
    specifically covered.
-   Normal wear and tear.
-   Consumable deterioration unless the product-specific warranty
    explicitly covers it.

The exact product warranty terms may contain additional exclusions.

## Warranty Claim Process

The standard warranty process is:

1.  Customer provides the order/invoice details.
2.  ElectroMart verifies product identity and warranty status.
3.  Support records the reported fault.
4.  Basic safe troubleshooting may be performed where appropriate.
5.  The case is routed to ElectroMart or the manufacturer depending on
    warranty responsibility.
6.  The product may require inspection or diagnosis.
7.  The authorized provider determines the applicable remedy.
8.  Repair, replacement, or another approved resolution is completed
    when the claim is accepted.

Support must not instruct customers to open, modify, or repair
electronic devices when doing so could create a safety risk or affect
warranty eligibility.

## Warranty Inspection and Diagnosis

Inspection may verify:

-   Product serial number.
-   Purchase/delivery date.
-   Warranty status.
-   Reported symptoms.
-   Physical condition.
-   Signs of liquid or accidental damage.
-   Previous unauthorized repair.
-   Product functionality.

A reported failure does not automatically establish that the failure is
covered.

## Warranty and Return Window

The standard ElectroMart return window is **7 calendar days from
delivery**.

If a customer requests a normal return after that period because they
changed their mind, the warranty policy does not extend the return
window.

If a product develops a qualifying functional fault after the
return/replacement period, the customer may use the warranty process
when the product is within its warranty period and the fault is covered.

## Warranty and Replacement Window

The standard ElectroMart replacement period is **7 calendar days from
delivery**.

A product that becomes defective after the replacement period should
normally be evaluated under warranty rather than treated as a late
standard replacement request.

However, a product-specific manufacturer DOA policy may provide a
different process. The product's applicable warranty record must be
checked.

## Repair vs Replacement

A warranty claim does not guarantee replacement.

The authorized warranty provider determines whether the appropriate
remedy is:

-   Repair.
-   Replacement.
-   Component replacement.
-   Another remedy permitted by the applicable warranty terms.

Replacement availability may affect the resolution.

## Warranty After Unauthorized Repair

Unauthorized repair, opening, modification, or alteration may invalidate
or limit warranty coverage where the applicable warranty terms exclude
such activity.

A customer should be advised to use an authorized service channel.

## Warranty Transfer

Warranty transferability depends on the product and manufacturer terms.

Customer support must not promise that a warranty can be transferred to
another person unless the applicable product terms confirm it.

## Warranty for Accessories

Accessories may have shorter warranty periods than the primary product.

Unless otherwise specified:

-   Chargers and power adapters: 6 months.
-   Other computer/mobile accessories: 6 months.

The product-specific warranty record takes precedence.

## Examples and Edge Cases

### Example 1: Laptop fails after 6 months

If the laptop has a 1-year warranty and the failure appears to be a
covered defect, the customer should use the warranty process. It is not
a standard return request.

### Example 2: Customer drops a laptop after 8 months

Accidental physical damage is generally excluded from warranty coverage.
The customer may need to use a paid repair or manufacturer
damage-service option if available.

### Example 3: Customer repaired the laptop at an unauthorized shop

Warranty coverage may be rejected or limited if unauthorized repair is
an exclusion under the applicable warranty terms.

### Example 4: Customer wants replacement after 12 days because the product is defective

The standard 7-day replacement window has expired. The product should be
evaluated under its warranty terms.

### Example 5: Product is under manufacturer warranty

ElectroMart can assist with the process, but the manufacturer controls
the warranty decision when manufacturer warranty applies.

### Example 6: Customer wants a refund because the product failed after 8 months

A warranty claim does not automatically create a refund entitlement. The
authorized warranty provider determines the remedy under the applicable
warranty terms.
