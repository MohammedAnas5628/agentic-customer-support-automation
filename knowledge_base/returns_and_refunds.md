# ElectroMart Returns and Refunds Policy

## Overview

This policy defines when a customer may return an eligible product, how
a return is processed, how inspection affects the outcome, and how
refunds are issued.

A **return** is the process of sending an eligible delivered product
back to ElectroMart.

A **refund** is the repayment of an eligible amount after the applicable
return, cancellation, payment, or investigation process has been
completed.

A return request does not automatically mean that a refund has already
been approved.

## Return Eligibility

Unless a product-specific exception applies, an eligible product may be
returned within **7 calendar days from the date of delivery**.

The product must normally:

-   Be the product originally supplied by ElectroMart.
-   Be in acceptable return condition.
-   Include the required accessories and components.
-   Include original packaging where the product category requires it.
-   Not show signs of misuse, unauthorized modification, or avoidable
    physical damage.
-   Pass the applicable return inspection.

The order record and product policy determine whether a particular item
is return-eligible.

## Return Window

The standard return window is **7 calendar days from delivery**.

The delivery date recorded in the order system is used to calculate the
return window.

If the seventh calendar day has passed, a standard return request is
normally not accepted unless a specific exception or escalation rule
applies.

A customer should not be promised an extension merely because the
request was made shortly after the deadline.

## Opened Products

Opening a product does not automatically make it non-returnable.

An opened product may still qualify for return when:

-   The product category permits opened returns.
-   The product has not been misused.
-   Required accessories and packaging are present where applicable.
-   The product passes inspection.

Products that cannot be safely resold or that have been activated,
personalized, altered, or materially used may be excluded according to
the applicable product rules.

## Non-Returnable Products

The following categories are generally non-returnable unless the issue
qualifies for replacement, warranty, or another approved exception:

-   Software licenses and activation codes after activation.
-   Downloadable digital products after delivery or activation.
-   Personalized or customized products.
-   Products damaged through customer misuse.
-   Products with serial numbers that do not match the order record.
-   Products with evidence of unauthorized modification or repair.
-   Products missing essential components when those components are
    required for inspection or resale.

A product that is non-returnable may still be covered by a warranty or a
damaged/defective-product process if the applicable conditions are met.

## Return Reasons

Common return reasons include:

-   Product no longer required, where the category permits
    change-of-mind returns.
-   Product does not meet the customer's expected requirements, where
    the category permits such returns.
-   Wrong product received.
-   Product received with a qualifying defect.
-   Product received damaged.

Damage or defect cases may be handled through replacement instead of a
standard change-of-mind return. See `cancellation_and_replacement.md`.

## Return Process

The standard return process is:

1.  Customer submits a return request within the applicable return
    window.
2.  ElectroMart verifies order and product eligibility.
3.  ElectroMart provides return instructions when the request qualifies.
4.  Product is collected or returned through the authorized return
    method.
5.  The product is inspected.
6.  ElectroMart determines whether the return conditions have been
    satisfied.
7.  An approved refund is initiated according to the applicable refund
    method.

Customers should not ship products to an unofficial address or third
party unless ElectroMart specifically provides that instruction.

## Inspection Requirements

Inspection may verify:

-   Product identity and serial number.
-   Physical condition.
-   Functional condition where applicable.
-   Included accessories.
-   Packaging.
-   Signs of misuse, liquid damage, tampering, or unauthorized repair.
-   Whether the reported issue matches the returned product.

A refund is not automatically due before the required inspection is
completed.

If inspection shows that the product does not meet return conditions,
the return may be rejected and the product may be sent back to the
customer according to the applicable support process.

## Refund Eligibility

A refund is issued only after the applicable cancellation, return,
payment, or investigation conditions have been satisfied.

For a standard product return, refund approval generally requires:

-   Valid return eligibility.
-   Product receipt by ElectroMart or its authorized return partner.
-   Successful inspection where inspection is required.

For a cancellation before shipment, a refund may be initiated without a
physical return because the product has not been delivered.

## Refund Methods

For prepaid orders, refunds are normally returned to the original
payment method.

Examples:

-   Credit card payment → refund to the applicable card/payment source.
-   Debit card payment → refund to the applicable payment source.
-   UPI payment → refund through the applicable UPI/payment route.
-   Net banking → refund through the applicable payment route.

For COD orders, approved refunds are normally transferred to the bank
account or payment method verified through ElectroMart's refund
workflow.

ElectroMart support must not request a customer's UPI PIN, card CVV,
password, or OTP to process a legitimate refund.

## Refund Timelines

After refund approval, ElectroMart initiates the refund according to the
payment method.

Typical processing timelines are:

  Refund method                      Typical timeline after approval
  -------------------------------- ---------------------------------
  UPI                                             1--3 business days
  Credit/Debit Card                               5--7 business days
  Net Banking                                     3--5 business days
  Verified bank transfer for COD                  3--5 business days

The customer's bank or payment provider may take additional time after
ElectroMart initiates the refund.

A refund marked as `Processed` means ElectroMart has completed its
refund-side action. It does not guarantee that the customer's bank has
already posted the amount.

## Refund Before Inspection

For a standard return requiring inspection, a customer cannot demand a
confirmed refund before the product is received and the required
inspection is completed.

Exceptional refunds may be approved through an authorized support or
escalation process when operational evidence supports the decision.

Support must not promise an exception without authorization.

## Partial Refunds

If only part of an order is eligible for refund, the refund applies only
to the approved item or amount.

Shipping charges, discounts, taxes, and other components may be
recalculated according to the applicable order and promotional rules.

Support should use the order and refund systems for the exact amount
rather than estimating it manually.

## Damaged or Defective Products

A product that arrives damaged or defective may qualify for replacement
or another remedy under `cancellation_and_replacement.md`.

A damaged or defective product is not automatically treated as a
standard change-of-mind return.

Customers should report delivery damage or an apparent defect as soon as
reasonably possible so ElectroMart can perform the appropriate
verification.

## Return After the Return Window

After the standard 7-calendar-day return window, a normal return is
generally unavailable.

If the product develops a functional fault after the return window, the
customer should use the warranty process where the product is covered by
warranty.

A warranty claim is not a way to bypass a return deadline for a simple
change-of-mind request.

## Examples and Edge Cases

### Example 1: Customer opened a laptop and wants to return it after 3 days

Opening the package does not automatically disqualify the return. The
request must still satisfy the product's return conditions and pass
inspection.

### Example 2: Customer asks for a refund before the returned laptop is inspected

A confirmed standard refund should not be promised before required
inspection is completed.

### Example 3: Customer wants to return a product after 10 days

The standard return window has expired. A normal return is not available
unless an authorized exception applies.

### Example 4: Customer's product develops a fault after 6 months

This is generally a warranty issue rather than a standard return. The
applicable warranty terms should be checked.

### Example 5: Customer received a damaged monitor

The customer should use the damaged-product/replacement process. The
replacement policy determines eligibility and verification.

### Example 6: Customer paid by UPI and the refund is marked processed

ElectroMart has completed its refund-side action. The customer should
allow the stated UPI processing window and contact support if the amount
remains missing after that period.
