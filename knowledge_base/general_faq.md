# ElectroMart General FAQ

## Overview

This FAQ covers general ElectroMart questions that do not require a
specialized policy or customer-specific backend lookup.

When a question belongs to a dedicated policy, that policy should be
treated as the authoritative source.

## What is ElectroMart?

ElectroMart is a fictional India-focused online electronics retailer
offering consumer electronics, computing products, smartphones,
accessories, networking equipment, and related products.

## Does ElectroMart operate in India?

Yes. ElectroMart's business and customer-support operations are designed
for customers in India.

Delivery is available only to serviceable Indian pincodes and may vary
by product and courier.

## What are ElectroMart's support hours?

Customer support is available:

-   Monday--Saturday: 9:00 AM--8:00 PM IST.
-   Sunday: 10:00 AM--6:00 PM IST.

Public holidays may affect support operations.

## Does ElectroMart deliver everywhere in India?

No. ElectroMart delivers to eligible serviceable pincodes.

Serviceability can vary by product, destination, courier coverage, and
operational conditions.

The delivery system is the source of truth for a specific pincode or
order.

## Can I track my order?

Yes. Once shipment tracking is available, customers can use the tracking
information associated with the order.

Customer-specific tracking information must be retrieved from the
order/shipment system.

See `shipping_and_delivery.md`.

## Can I cancel my order?

Cancellation depends on the current order status.

Customers can request cancellation while an order is in eligible
pre-shipment stages, subject to backend validation.

Cancellation is not permitted after the order has entered `PACKED`,
`SHIPPED`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, or `DELIVERED`.

See `cancellation_and_replacement.md`.

## Can I return an opened product?

Opening a product does not automatically make it ineligible for return.

The product must be return-eligible and satisfy the applicable condition
and inspection requirements.

See `returns_and_refunds.md`.

## What is the standard return window?

The standard return window is **7 calendar days from delivery** for
eligible products.

Product-specific exceptions may apply.

## What is the standard replacement window?

The standard replacement period is **7 calendar days from delivery** for
eligible damaged, defective, incorrect, or qualifying missing-component
cases.

See `cancellation_and_replacement.md`.

## What happens if a product develops a fault after several months?

If the product is within its applicable warranty period and the fault is
covered, the customer should use the warranty process.

A late fault is not automatically eligible for a standard return or
replacement.

See `warranty.md`.

## Does ElectroMart offer Cash on Delivery?

COD is available for eligible orders, products, and pincodes.

The checkout system determines whether COD is available for a particular
order.

## Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

An address change may be possible before shipment if the new address is
serviceable and the authorized workflow permits the change.

After shipment, ElectroMart cannot normally change the delivery address.

See `shipping_and_delivery.md`.

## What if my order says delivered but I did not receive it?

The case should be investigated.

Customers should first check household members, building
reception/security, and authorized nearby recipients where appropriate.

Support must not automatically promise a refund or replacement solely
because the tracking status says delivered.

## How do I get a GST invoice?

Eligible orders receive an invoice through the applicable billing/order
process.

Business customers should provide accurate GST information before
invoice finalization where possible.

See `invoices_and_tax.md`.

## Can I get help from a human?

Yes. Customers can request human support, and issues requiring human
judgment, investigation, security review, or policy exceptions can be
escalated.

See `customer_support.md` and `escalation_rules.md`.

## What information should I never share with support?

Never share:

-   Passwords.
-   OTPs.
-   UPI PINs.
-   CVVs.
-   Full payment-card credentials.

See `account_and_security.md`.

## Does the catalog show live stock?

No. The static product catalog is not the source of truth for current
inventory.

Current stock must be retrieved from the inventory system.

## Does the catalog show today's price?

No. Product pricing is dynamic.

Current price must be retrieved from the product/pricing system.
