# ElectroMart Company Handbook

## Overview

ElectroMart is a fictional India-focused electronics e-commerce company
that sells consumer electronics, computing products, mobile devices,
accessories, home electronics, and related products through its online
platform.

ElectroMart's customer-facing policies are designed around four
principles:

1.  Give customers clear and accurate information.
2.  Protect customer payments, accounts, and personal information.
3.  Apply published policies consistently while allowing defined
    escalation for exceptional cases.
4.  Never promise an outcome that requires verification from an order,
    payment, inventory, courier, or support system.

This handbook provides general company information. Product, delivery,
return, cancellation, replacement, and warranty rules are defined in
their dedicated policies.

## Mission

ElectroMart's mission is to make electronics shopping in India simple,
transparent, and dependable by combining a broad product selection with
clear policies and responsive customer support.

## Business Model

ElectroMart operates primarily as an online electronics retailer.

ElectroMart may sell products:

-   Directly as ElectroMart inventory.
-   Through approved marketplace or brand-supply arrangements.
-   Through authorized manufacturers or distributors.

The applicable seller or warranty responsibility is determined by the
product's order and product records. Customer support must not assume
that every product has the same warranty provider.

## Products and Services

ElectroMart may offer:

-   Laptops and computers.
-   Smartphones and tablets.
-   Monitors and displays.
-   Audio products.
-   Computer and mobile accessories.
-   Networking equipment.
-   Smart-home and consumer electronics.
-   Selected appliances and electronic peripherals.

Customer-facing services include:

-   Online product discovery and ordering.
-   Delivery tracking.
-   Return requests for eligible products.
-   Refund processing.
-   Eligible order cancellation.
-   Eligible product replacement.
-   Warranty guidance and claim assistance.
-   Customer support ticket creation.
-   Human escalation for defined cases.

Live price, stock availability, order status, payment status, refund
status, and customer-specific eligibility are transactional data. They
must be obtained from the appropriate backend system rather than
inferred from this handbook.

## Operating Locations and Delivery Coverage

ElectroMart operates its customer support and fulfillment activities
within India.

ElectroMart supports deliveries to eligible Indian serviceable pincodes.
Serviceability can vary by product, courier, destination, and delivery
conditions.

Major supported metropolitan and regional markets may include:

-   Hyderabad
-   Bengaluru
-   Chennai
-   Mumbai
-   Pune
-   Delhi NCR
-   Kolkata
-   Ahmedabad
-   Jaipur
-   Lucknow
-   Other serviceable Indian cities, towns, and pincodes

The presence of a city on this list does not guarantee that every
pincode or every product is serviceable. The delivery system is the
source of truth for a specific order or pincode.

ElectroMart does not promise delivery to a destination merely because it
is within India.

## General Customer Rules

Customers are expected to:

-   Provide accurate delivery and contact information.
-   Use payment methods they are authorized to use.
-   Keep account credentials secure.
-   Follow product-use and safety instructions.
-   Keep invoices and order information available when required for
    support.
-   Provide truthful information when reporting damage, defects, missing
    items, payment problems, or delivery issues.
-   Cooperate with required return, replacement, or warranty
    inspections.

ElectroMart may restrict or escalate requests involving suspected fraud,
repeated policy abuse, unauthorized payment activity, or attempts to
bypass security controls.

## Policy Hierarchy

Customer-specific transactional decisions must be based on backend
records and validated business rules.

The general priority is:

1.  Authentication and authorization controls.
2.  Backend order, payment, inventory, shipment, and ticket records.
3.  The applicable dedicated ElectroMart policy.
4.  General company information in this handbook.

A customer-facing agent must not override a backend result by relying
only on a general statement in the knowledge base.

For example, this handbook does not determine whether a particular order
can be cancelled. The cancellation policy defines the general rule,
while the order system determines the actual order status and
eligibility.

## Customer Communication Principles

ElectroMart support should:

-   State policy conditions clearly.
-   Distinguish confirmed information from information that requires
    verification.
-   Give applicable time periods in calendar days or business days as
    explicitly defined.
-   Avoid promising refunds, replacements, or cancellations before the
    required checks are complete.
-   Tell customers when a human review is required.

ElectroMart support must not request or disclose sensitive
authentication information such as passwords, OTPs, CVVs, or full
payment-card credentials.
