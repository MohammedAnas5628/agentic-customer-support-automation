# ElectroMart Product Catalog

## Overview

This document provides representative product knowledge for
ElectroMart's fictional catalog. It is designed for product-information
retrieval and customer-support questions.

Product price and inventory are dynamic business data. The catalog
provides product attributes and reference information, but a
customer-specific question about current price, stock, order
availability, or delivery eligibility must be verified through the
appropriate backend system.

## Catalog Conventions

Each product has:

-   Product ID.
-   Product name.
-   Category.
-   Key specifications.
-   Included items.
-   Standard warranty reference.
-   Compatibility information where relevant.

The Product ID is the stable identifier used by ElectroMart's product
systems.

## Product Warranty Reference

Unless a product-specific record states otherwise:

-   Laptops and desktops: 1 year.
-   Smartphones and tablets: 1 year.
-   Monitors: 1 year.
-   Networking equipment: 1 year.
-   Audio products: 1 year.
-   Chargers and power adapters: 6 months.
-   Other computer/mobile accessories: 6 months.

Warranty responsibility may belong to ElectroMart or the manufacturer.
See `warranty.md`.

## Price and Availability Rules

The prices in this document should not be treated as live prices.

Current price must be obtained from the product/order system.

Current inventory must be obtained from the inventory system.

A product appearing in this catalog does not guarantee that it is
currently in stock or deliverable to a particular pincode.

## Product Comparison Guidance

The Knowledge Agent may compare documented specifications.

It must not claim that one product is objectively "best" unless the
customer specifies criteria such as price, performance, portability,
display quality, or intended use.

Recommendations should be based only on available product information
and should clearly identify when information is insufficient.
