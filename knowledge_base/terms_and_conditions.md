# ElectroMart Terms and Conditions

## Overview

These fictional terms describe the general rules governing use of
ElectroMart's online services.

They are designed as project documentation for the ElectroMart
customer-support system and are not a substitute for legal advice or a
real-world consumer contract.

## Acceptance of Terms

By using ElectroMart's online services, a customer agrees to follow the
applicable terms, policies, and reasonable usage requirements associated
with the service.

Dedicated policies may provide additional rules for specific services
such as delivery, returns, refunds, warranty, payments, and account
security.

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

-   Providing accurate account and delivery information.
-   Using payment methods they are authorized to use.
-   Maintaining account security.
-   Reviewing product information before purchase.
-   Providing accurate information during support, return, replacement,
    warranty, and payment investigations.
-   Following applicable product-use and safety instructions.

Customers must not knowingly provide false information to obtain an
unauthorized refund, replacement, discount, cancellation, or other
benefit.

## Account Responsibility

Customers are responsible for protecting their authentication
credentials.

ElectroMart support will not request passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, CVVs, or
full payment-card credentials.

Customers should promptly report suspected unauthorized account access.

See `account_and_security.md`.

## Product Information

ElectroMart aims to provide accurate product information.

Product specifications may vary by model or product revision. The
product record and applicable product listing are authoritative for the
specific product.

Current price, inventory, delivery availability, and customer-specific
offers are dynamic information and must be verified through the
applicable system.

## Pricing and Order Acceptance

Displaying a product does not guarantee that the product will remain
available at the displayed price until purchase.

An order becomes subject to ElectroMart's order-confirmation and payment
processes.

If a material pricing, inventory, or system error is identified,
ElectroMart may review the order and take an authorized corrective
action consistent with applicable law and customer communication
requirements.

The AI agent must not independently cancel or alter an order because it
believes a price is incorrect.

## Payments

Customers must use payment methods they are authorized to use.

Payment confirmation is determined by ElectroMart's payment system and
applicable payment provider.

Payment disputes are handled according to `payments.md` and, where
required, `escalation_rules.md`.

## Delivery

Delivery is available only to eligible serviceable locations.

Delivery estimates are estimates based on the applicable shipping method
and operational conditions.

Courier delays, weather, public holidays, transportation disruptions,
and other operational events may affect delivery.

The detailed delivery policy is defined in `shipping_and_delivery.md`.

## Cancellation

Cancellation is subject to the order's current status and the applicable
cancellation rules.

Customers cannot assume that an order can be cancelled simply because it
has not yet been delivered.

The dedicated cancellation policy is defined in
`cancellation_and_replacement.md`.

## Returns and Refunds

Returns are available only for eligible products under the applicable
return conditions.

A return request does not automatically guarantee a refund.

Refunds are processed only after the applicable conditions, inspections,
and backend validations have been completed.

See `returns_and_refunds.md`.

## Replacement

Replacement is a separate remedy from return and refund.

A replacement may be available for qualifying damaged, defective,
incorrect, or missing-component cases within the applicable replacement
process.

Replacement is subject to verification, inspection, availability, and
product-specific conditions.

See `cancellation_and_replacement.md`.

## Warranty

Warranty coverage applies only to qualifying defects and failures
covered by the applicable warranty terms.

The warranty provider may be ElectroMart or the manufacturer.

A warranty does not automatically guarantee a refund or replacement.

See `warranty.md`.

## Promotions

Coupons, discounts, and promotions are subject to their published terms.

ElectroMart may limit one-time offers, minimum-order requirements,
category restrictions, payment-method requirements, and stacking.

The promotion system determines current eligibility.

See `offers_and_discounts.md`.

## Invoices and Tax

Invoices are generated according to the applicable billing process.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate billing and tax
information.

GST and other tax information shown on an invoice should be treated as
the authoritative transaction record.

See `invoices_and_tax.md`.

## Prohibited Use

Customers must not use ElectroMart's services to:

-   Commit fraud.
-   Abuse promotional systems.
-   Gain unauthorized access to accounts or systems.
-   Submit knowingly false claims.
-   Attempt to obtain another customer's information.
-   Interfere with the normal operation of the service.
-   Circumvent security or authorization controls.

Suspected abuse may be reviewed and escalated.

## Fraud and Abuse

ElectroMart may investigate suspected fraudulent or abusive activity.

Examples include:

-   Repeated false damage claims.
-   Manipulation of return or refund processes.
-   Coupon abuse.
-   Unauthorized payment activity.
-   Account sharing intended to bypass restrictions.
-   Attempts to obtain customer data without authorization.

Investigation does not automatically establish wrongdoing; appropriate
evidence and authorized review are required.

## Policy Exceptions

Customer support cannot create an exception merely because a customer
requests one.

An exception must be authorized through the applicable support or
escalation process.

The AI agent must not promise an exception before authorization is
confirmed.

## Third-Party Services

ElectroMart may rely on third parties such as:

-   Courier providers.
-   Payment providers.
-   Manufacturers.
-   Authorized service centers.

Third-party processing times or decisions may be outside ElectroMart's
direct control.

ElectroMart support should clearly distinguish between an ElectroMart
decision and a third-party decision.

## Service Availability

Online services may occasionally be unavailable because of maintenance,
technical problems, network issues, or external service disruptions.

A technical issue does not authorize the AI agent to invent transaction
results.

When a required backend system is unavailable, the agent should explain
that verification is temporarily unavailable and create or escalate a
support case when appropriate.

## Changes to Policies

ElectroMart may update operational policies when business processes
change.

Each knowledge-base policy contains an effective date and version.

For customer-specific decisions, the applicable policy version and
transaction records should be considered according to the system's
policy-management rules.

## Limitation of Automated Support

AI-generated support responses are intended to assist customers using
approved company information and tools.

The AI agent must not:

-   Invent policies.
-   Invent transaction results.
-   Claim that an action was completed without backend confirmation.
-   Override authorization controls.
-   Reveal protected customer information.
-   Make unsupported legal or financial commitments.

Cases requiring human judgment must be escalated.

## Disputes and Human Review

Customers may request human review of disputes that cannot be resolved
through standard support.

Human review does not guarantee a particular outcome.

The final decision should be based on applicable policy, transaction
records, evidence, and authorized business review.
