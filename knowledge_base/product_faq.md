# ElectroMart Product FAQ

## Overview

This FAQ provides common product, compatibility, specification, usage,
and comparison guidance for ElectroMart's representative product
catalog.

The Product Catalog contains the authoritative reference specifications.
Current price, stock, and customer-specific order information must be
retrieved from backend systems.

## Laptops

### Is the ElectroBook Pro 14 suitable for programming?

Yes. The ElectroBook Pro 14 has 16 GB RAM, a 512 GB SSD, and a Core
i5-class processor, making it suitable for common programming,
development, IDE, database, and web-development workloads.

Very demanding machine-learning training or professional GPU workloads
should not be assumed to be supported because the product uses
integrated graphics.

### Is 16 GB RAM enough for development?

For general software development, web development, Python development,
IDE use, and typical academic projects, 16 GB is generally suitable.

Workloads involving large virtual machines, extensive containers, or
heavy local model training may require more memory or dedicated
hardware.

### Does the ElectroBook Pro 14 include a charger?

Yes. The laptop includes its power adapter.

### Does the ElectroBook Air 15 support programming?

Yes. It is suitable for basic programming, web development, study, and
general development workloads. Its 8 GB RAM makes it less suitable than
the ElectroBook Pro 14 for heavier multitasking.

## Smartphones

### Does the ElectroPhone X5 support 5G?

Yes. The ElectroPhone X5 supports 5G. Actual 5G service depends on the
customer's network operator, plan, coverage, and compatible bands.

### Does the ElectroPhone Lite 5G support dual SIM?

The ElectroPhone Lite 5G supports dual-SIM operation as specified in its
product configuration.

### Does the ElectroPhone X5 include a charging adapter?

No charging adapter should be assumed to be included. The documented
package includes the smartphone, USB-C cable, and documentation unless
the current product listing states otherwise.

## Monitors

### Can the ElectroView 24 FHD connect to a laptop?

Yes, if the laptop provides a compatible HDMI or DisplayPort connection,
directly or through an appropriate adapter.

### Can the ElectroView 27 QHD be used for office work?

Yes. Its 27-inch QHD display is suitable for productivity, development,
browsing, and general desktop use.

### Does a monitor need to have built-in Wi-Fi?

No. A monitor normally receives video from a compatible source such as a
computer or streaming device. Wi-Fi is not required for the monitor
itself unless a specific smart-monitor feature requires it.

## Chargers and USB-C

### Can the ElectroCharge 65W USB-C adapter charge every USB-C laptop?

No. USB-C connector compatibility alone does not guarantee charging
compatibility.

The laptop must support USB-C charging and accept compatible power
specifications. The customer should check the laptop manufacturer's
charging requirements.

### Can a phone use the 65W USB-C adapter?

A compatible phone can use the adapter if it supports the required USB-C
charging standard. The phone controls the power it draws within its
supported limits.

## Networking

### Does ElectroMesh AC1200 support both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz?

Yes. It is a dual-band router supporting 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi.

### Can the router provide the same speed on every device?

No. Actual speed depends on the internet plan, device capabilities,
distance, interference, network configuration, and other factors.

## Product Comparison

### Which is better for programming: ElectroBook Pro 14 or ElectroBook Air 15?

For heavier programming and multitasking, the ElectroBook Pro 14 is the
stronger choice because it has 16 GB RAM compared with 8 GB on the
ElectroBook Air 15.

The ElectroBook Air 15 may be appropriate when the customer prioritizes
basic productivity and study workloads.

### Which monitor should I choose for higher resolution?

The ElectroView 27 QHD provides 2560 × 1440 resolution, while the
ElectroView 24 FHD provides 1920 × 1080.

The 27 QHD is therefore the higher-resolution option.

## Compatibility Rules

Customers should verify compatibility using:

-   Connector type.
-   Supported charging or power specification.
-   Required software or operating-system support.
-   Display input/output standards.
-   Manufacturer requirements.
-   Product-specific limitations.

A physically compatible connector does not always mean that two devices
are functionally compatible.

## Product Information vs Live Data

The Knowledge Agent can answer documented specification questions from
this FAQ and `product_catalog.md`.

It must use backend tools for:

-   Current stock.
-   Current price.
-   Customer-specific discounts.
-   Order availability.
-   Delivery estimates.
-   Customer-specific warranty status.
-   Product availability at a particular pincode.

## Product FAQ Edge Cases

### Customer asks whether a USB-C charger will work with an unknown laptop

Do not guarantee compatibility without the laptop model or charging
specification.

### Customer asks whether an item is currently in stock

Do not infer stock from the catalog. Use the inventory system.

### Customer asks today's product price

Do not use a static catalog price as a live price. Use the
product/pricing system.

### Customer asks whether a product is "best"

Ask or infer the relevant criteria only when sufficient information is
available, and avoid unsupported claims.
